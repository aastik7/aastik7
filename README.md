## Featured projects

### 🧠 [Multi-Agent Resume Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/aastik7/Multi-Agent-Resume-Intelligence-Platform)

A full-stack AI workflow that turns a resume and job description into a skill-alignment report, resume rewrite, and tailored cover letter.

- Six **LangGraph** stages with **Pydantic-validated outputs**.
- Exact and semantic skill matching with an explainable scoring breakdown.
- **Next.js + FastAPI**, with live progress streamed through **SSE**.

**Explore:** [Architecture, setup, tests, and trade-offs](https://github.com/aastik7/Multi-Agent-Resume-Intelligence-Platform#system-architecture)

### 📚 [Bank Assist — Corrective RAG](https://github.com/aastik7/AI-RAG-Banking)

A PDF-grounded banking knowledge assistant that shows where its answers come from.

- **LangGraph + Gemini + ChromaDB** retrieval with page-level citations.
- Bounded query rewriting and abstention when evidence is insufficient.
- Idempotent ingestion, pipeline traces, and per-node timings.

**Explore:** [System design and retrieval decisions](https://github.com/aastik7/AI-RAG-Banking#system-design)

### 🛍️ [HippoKart — Digital Marketplace](https://github.com/aastik7/hippokart)

A **Next.js + TypeScript** marketplace with authentication, seller listings, a persistent cart, and admin product review. Uses **tRPC** for typed APIs and **Payload CMS** for content and authentication.

**Explore:** [Features and local setup](https://github.com/aastik7/hippokart#features)
